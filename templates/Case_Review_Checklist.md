# Simulated case review checklist

- [ ] State the fictional alert and customer profile.
- [ ] Preserve observed facts and dates separately from interpretations.
- [ ] Identify specific unusual activity and relevant customer context.
- [ ] Check for benign explanations and missing information.
- [ ] Document the rationale for clearing, requesting information, or escalating.
- [ ] Avoid treating training thresholds as legal requirements.
- [ ] Identify limitations and do not claim a formal SAR/STR filing.
